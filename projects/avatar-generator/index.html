Avatar Generator

Turn a nickname into a repeatable illustrated avatar, with three styles to explore.

Leave the name blank for a fresh username and avatar each time. A custom name repeats the same avatar for the selected style.

Avatars load from DiceBear; the nickname is sent as a seed, so avoid personal information. Lorelei by Lisa Wischofsky and Pixel Art by DiceBear use CC0; Bottts by Pablo Stanley is free for personal and commercial use. See credits and style licenses. Nothing is saved here.
