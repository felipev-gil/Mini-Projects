Currency Converter

Convert an amount between currencies using the latest published exchange rates.

Enter an amount from 0 to 999,999,999.

Choose your currencies and select Convert amount.

Rates provided by ExchangeRate-API . The update time is shown with each result; rates are not a live trading feed.

Results are estimates and exclude bank fees. Only the base currency is sent to the rate provider. Your selected currencies are remembered in this browser.
