Kanban Board

Organize tasks into To do, In progress, and Done using drag and drop or simple move controls.

Drag a card to a column, or choose a destination from its Move to menu.

Tasks are saved only in this browser. There is no account or synchronization. If storage is blocked, the board still works for this visit. Drag and drop is optional; every task has keyboard and touch controls.
