Country Explorer

Search countries and territories by name or country code, and explore capitals, languages, and currencies.

Search runs locally against a bundled snapshot of mledoze/countries (November 9, 2026), licensed under ODbL 1.0. It includes countries and territories. Values are reference data, not live updates; no search text is sent to an external service.
