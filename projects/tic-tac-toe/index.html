Tic Tac Toe

Two players, one device. Take turns placing X and O; connect three in a row to win.

Player X’s turn.

X starts each game. A row, column, or diagonal of three matching marks wins. A full board without a winner is a draw.

Use Tab or the arrow keys to move between cells, then Enter or Space to place your mark. Select New game to reset the board.
